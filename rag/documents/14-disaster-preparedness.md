# Weather-related disaster preparedness

> Source: National Disaster Management Authority (India) public preparedness guidance

## An emergency kit

Keep in one accessible bag: drinking water for three days, non-perishable food, essential medicines with prescriptions, a torch with spare batteries, a power bank, a first aid kit, a whistle, copies of identity documents in a waterproof pouch, some cash in small notes, and a battery or hand-crank radio.

## A family plan

Agree on a meeting point, an out-of-area contact everyone can call, and who collects children from school. Save local emergency numbers, and note that 112 is the single emergency number in India. Practise the plan with everyone in the household, including the route to the nearest shelter or higher ground.

## Before the season

Clear roof drains and gutters before the monsoon, check that trees near the building are pruned, repair loose roofing sheets and signage, and note whether your locality has a history of waterlogging. Keep insurance and property documents digitised.

## Staying informed

Rely on official sources during an event: IMD bulletins and nowcasts, state disaster management authority advisories, district administration announcements and local radio. Automated app assessments, including this one, are useful for planning but do not replace an official warning.

## Helping others

Check on elderly neighbours, people living alone, people with disabilities and outdoor workers before and during extreme weather. Most early rescue in Indian disasters is done by neighbours before formal agencies arrive.
