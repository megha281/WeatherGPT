# Heavy rainfall and urban flooding

> Source: National Disaster Management Authority (India) urban flooding guidelines, public guidance

## Why cities flood

Urban flooding happens when rainfall exceeds the capacity of drains, or when drains are blocked by silt and solid waste. Paved surfaces prevent infiltration, so a much larger share of rainfall becomes surface runoff than in rural areas. Flooding can therefore occur within minutes of intense rain beginning, and can happen far from any river.

## Warning signs

Rapidly rising water in streets, water backing up from drains, sustained rainfall over several hours, and upstream rainfall in the catchment even when it is dry locally.

## Safety actions before heavy rain

- Charge phones and power banks, and keep a torch accessible.
- Store drinking water, since supply and treatment can be disrupted.
- Move vehicles out of basements and low-lying parking.
- Keep important documents in a waterproof bag on an upper shelf.
- Identify the nearest higher ground and the safest route to it.

## During flooding

Do not walk or drive through moving water. About 15 cm of moving water can knock an adult off balance, and about 60 cm can float most cars. Floodwater hides open manholes, live cables and debris. Avoid contact with electrical fittings if water has entered the building, and switch off the mains at the board if it is safe and dry to reach.

## After flooding

Assume floodwater is contaminated. Boil or disinfect drinking water until authorities confirm supply is safe, discard soaked food, and get electrical wiring checked before switching supply back on. Watch for waterborne illness in the days that follow.
